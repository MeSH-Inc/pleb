Lock (computer science)

Edit links

In computer science, a lock or mutex is a synchronization primitive used to enforce mutual exclusion on shared resources.

Types

[edit]

Generally, locks are advisory locks, where each thread cooperates by acquiring the lock before accessing data.

Granularity

[edit]

Finer lock granularity increases concurrency at the cost of overhead.
